Getting Started

Click and drag on the canvas to draw. Use the tool palette on the left to switch between drawing and modification tools.

Hold Command + drag to create horizontal or vertical guides. Or hold Command + drag diagonally to the lower right to zoom in or upper left to zoom out.

Use the pointers snapping capability to reference geometry.

Navigation

  • Scroll wheel to zoom in/out
  • Hold Space and drag to pan
  • Use the hand tool for continuous panning

Selection

  • Click to select a shape
  • Drag to marquee select
  • Hold Shift and click to toggle selection
  • Hold Shift and marquee to add/remove from selection

Snapping

The drafting assistant automatically generates guide lines from snap points (endpoints, midpoints, centers, tangents). Guides appear as red dashed lines.
